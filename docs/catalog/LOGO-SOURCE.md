# Brand logo source

184 SVG/PNG assets were downloaded from https://github.com/vehiclespecs/brand-logos on November 11, 2026 and are stored in `public/car-logos`. The downloaded archive SHA-256 is recorded in `SNAPSHOT.json`. These are identification assets, not newly generated manufacturer trademarks.
